I am an AI Researcher and Applied Mathematician. My research investigates how we can use deep mathematical frameworks—such as representation learning, sequence modeling, and multiresolution analysis—to understand and reconstruct complex, high-dimensional data. While my roots are deeply established in computer graphics and computer vision, I am looking towards the intersection of these fields with computational neuroscience and cognitive modeling.

Currently, I am a Research Scientist at Hand Talk in the Foundation Models Group, where I design multimodal architectures that map continuous human motion into discrete semantic structures. I recently earned my PhD in Mathematics from IMPA 🇧🇷, where my thesis focused on Implicit Neural Representations (INRs) for continuous media signals. In 2022, I was a Research Scientist Intern at Reality Labs Research (Meta) in Pittsburgh, PA 🇺🇸, where I developed generative models and differentiable optimization techniques for the 3D reconstruction and registration of complex physiological systems.

I am also the co-creator of the YouTube channel [Programação Dinâmica](http://youtube.com/@pgdinamica), where I talk about programming, data science, artificial intelligence, and scientific outreach. The channel has garnered over 230K subscribers, was part of the [YouTube Black Voices Fund class of 2022](https://blog.youtube/intl/pt-br/news-and-events/brasil-fundo-vozes-negras-2022/), and has been recognized by YouTube as a [Creator of Impact](https://blog.youtube/intl/pt-br/news-and-events/criadores-de-impacto-ampliando-horizontes-e-fortalecendo-a-comunidade/) in 2024 and 2025.

My academic foundation spans a Master's degree in Mathematics from IMPA, focusing on implicit surface reconstruction, and a Bachelor's degree in Computer Engineering from the Military Institute of Engineering (IME), where I developed a strong background in formal languages and software architecture.

Whether it is building robust computational pipelines for artificial systems or exploring the algorithms of the human mind, I am driven by hard problems that require creative, interdisciplinary solutions.
